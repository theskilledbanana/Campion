1. Adding and Subtracting Decimals

When adding or subtracting decimals, the most important step is to align the decimal points. Fill any empty spaces with zeros so that the numbers have the same number of decimal places.

Example: 12.45 + 3.7 → 12.45 + 3.70 = 16.15

2. Key Fraction Rules

Adding Fractions

Find a common denominator before adding the numerators.

Multiplying Fractions

Multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators.

Mixed Numbers

Convert to improper fractions before performing calculations.

Simplifying

Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor.

3. Order of Operations (BIDMAS)

Always follow the correct order to solve expressions: Brackets, Indices, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction. Multiplication and Division have equal priority and are solved left to right.

Example walk-through:

10 + (2 × 3)² - 5

  • Brackets: 10 + (6)² - 5
  • Indices: 10 + 36 - 5
  • Addition/Subtraction: 46 - 5 = 41

4. Directed Numbers (Integers)

Integers include all whole numbers, their opposites, and zero. When adding a negative number, it is the same as subtracting a positive number.

Rules for Multiplication

-5 - 3 = -8°C
